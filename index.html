About Us / CEO Biography

Patrick "Pat" Mackaronis

Chief Executive Officer, Global Trading Partners

Over a decade of experience in Fuel & Petroleum, Global Trade, Logistics, and Import Export Transactions.

Pat Mackaronis started Global Trading Partners in 2019, after over a decade of experience in Fuel & Petroleum, Global Trade, Logistics, and Import Export Transactions. He began his career in 2006 as a fuel trading consultant for some of the larger US major oil companies.

He led the company into the PPE and Medical Supply industry in 2019/2020, and also owns and operates Mack Construction, a heavy construction company in New York City.
